# Benchmarks and datasets

Nothing is downloaded unless an experiment config (or `routeguard prepare-data`) asks for it.
Public datasets are loaded from the Hugging Face Hub at **pinned revisions** and converted to the
RouteGuard item schema. `routeguard prepare-data --config <experiment> --out data/` writes local
JSONL snapshots that the `jsonl` loader can read offline.

## Core suite (`benchmarks/configs/core_suite.yaml`)

| Loader | Dataset (Hub id) | Task / category | Languages used | Items (limit per language) | License (from dataset card) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `belebele` | `facebook/belebele` | reading comprehension, 4-way MC | en, kk, ru, fa (Iranian Persian, `pes_Arab`) | 150 | CC-BY-SA-4.0 |
| `sib200` | `Davlan/sib200` | topic classification, 7-way MC | en, kk, ru, fa (`pes_Arab`), **Dari (`prs_Arab`)** | 100 | CC-BY-SA-4.0 |
| `global_mmlu` | `CohereLabs/Global-MMLU` | knowledge / science, 4-way MC | en, ru, fa | 100 | Apache-2.0 |
| `mgsm` | `juletxara/mgsm` | grade-school math, numeric | en, ru | 150 | CC-BY-SA-4.0 |
| `mbpp` | `google-research-datasets/mbpp` (sanitized) | Python coding, execution | en | 150 | CC-BY-4.0 |
| `nq_open` | `google-research-datasets/nq_open` | open-domain factual QA, short text | en | 150 | CC-BY-SA-3.0 |
| `arc` | `allenai/ai2_arc` (ARC-Challenge) | science reasoning, MC | en | 150 | CC-BY-SA-4.0 |

Also available: `gsm8k` (`openai/gsm8k`, MIT). It is excluded from the core suite because the
English MGSM items are a verbatim subset of the GSM8K test set, and using both would place
identical questions in different splits.

The licenses above are the ones declared on the dataset cards at the pinned revisions. The
datasets are downloaded by the user, not redistributed with this repository, and each record keeps
its `source` and `license` fields. Check the dataset cards yourself before any redistribution.

**Parallel items.** Belebele, SIB-200, Global-MMLU and MGSM are parallel across languages. Items
share a `parallel_id`, per-language limits select the *same* underlying items in every language,
and splits keep all translations of an item on the same side. This enables paired cross-lingual
comparisons and the `language_related_failure` label in the error analysis.

## Scoring

Deterministic whenever ground truth allows (`evaluation/scoring.py`):

- numeric: canonical number equality (commas, Persian and Arabic-Indic digits, relative tolerance 1e-6);
- choice: option letter;
- code: execution of the dataset's tests (for MBPP these are the tests shown in the prompt, which
  is the standard MBPP protocol);
- text: `contains` (default): a normalised gold alias appears in the prediction *and* the
  prediction is at most 8 tokens longer, so listing many candidates is not rewarded. `em` and `f1`
  are also available. EM and token-F1 are always reported.

## Smoke fixtures (`benchmarks/samples/`)

`smoke.jsonl` (74 items), `rag_passages.jsonl` (8 items) and `tool_use.jsonl` (10 cases) are
**software-test fixtures**, released under CC0. They are templated arithmetic, simple facts and
tiny coding tasks, written by the maintainers in English, Kazakh, Russian and Persian, and
generated by `scripts/make_smoke_fixture.py`. The non-English templates have not been reviewed by
native speakers. They are used by CI and the simulated demo and **must not be used to report
results**.

## Split protocol

`splits: {train, calibration, test}` fractions, stratified by `stratify_by` keys (default
`[source, language]` in the core suite), group-aware by `parallel_id`, and seeded. Each seed gives
a different partition; the item pool is fixed across seeds (subsampling is seed-independent).

## RAG and tool-use evaluations

- `routeguard rag-eval --config experiments/rag/belebele_rag.yaml` turns Belebele into an
  open-book task: questions are asked *without* their passage, and BM25 retrieves from all passages
  of the same language. The report separates retrieval failures (gold passage not retrieved) from
  generation failures (retrieved but wrong), and reports answers produced without evidence,
  groundedness and citation support, per language. Groundedness and citation support are lexical
  proxies, not entailment judgements.
- `routeguard tool-eval --config experiments/tools/tool_use_hf.yaml` runs a text-protocol tool
  agent (calculator, unit converter) on `benchmarks/samples/tool_use.jsonl` and labels missing,
  unnecessary, wrong-tool and malformed calls, ignored, contradicted and fabricated results. With
  10 cases this is a probe of the evaluation machinery, not a benchmark. Add cases in the same
  schema for real studies.
